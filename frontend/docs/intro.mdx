Welcome to "Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics"! This book is designed to guide you through the exciting world of robotics, artificial intelligence, and the unique challenges and opportunities presented by humanoid platforms. Over the course of several modules, we will cover everything from the foundational software architecture to advanced control, perception, and intelligent decision-making for robots that interact with our physical world.

## Quarter Goals

This quarter, our primary goals include:

*   **Establishing a robust ROS 2 foundation:** Understanding the core communication mechanisms and development practices.
*   **Integrating Pythonic AI agents:** Bridging high-level intelligence with low-level robot control.
*   **Mastering robot description formats:** Accurately modeling complex humanoid structures.
*   **Developing advanced perception systems:** Enabling robots to "see" and understand their environment.
*   **Implementing intelligent control strategies:** Allowing humanoids to move and act autonomously.

## Module 1: The Robotic Nervous System (ROS 2)

We begin our journey with **Module 1: The Robotic Nervous System (ROS 2)**, where we will dive deep into the heart of modern robotics software. This module will equip you with the essential tools and concepts to build and program your own robotic systems.
